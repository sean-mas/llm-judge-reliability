       label           most-severe   majority   delta
       Consistent              175        175      +0
       Benign                   64         88     +24
       Questionable             72         78      +6
       Unwanted                439        409     -30
       -> most-severe  binary FAIL = 575/750 =  76.7%
       -> majority     binary FAIL = 575/750 =  76.7%
     Both rules give the same binary split, because they only move items
     between Benign/Questionable/Unwanted, all of which are FAIL under the
     strict rule. The aggregation choice is therefore not load-bearing.
     Majority vote is UNDEFINED on 77 items (exactly 2 annotators, split 1-1).
     Defined on the 180 items with 3+ annotators. Hence most-severe as primary.
